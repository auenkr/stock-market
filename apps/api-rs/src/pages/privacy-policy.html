This policy explains how Ledgerline handles information when you use its free financial data API, MCP server, CLI, and documentation.

Information we handle

The service does not require an account, login, API key, or payment. Requests contain the company symbols, screening queries, and other parameters you submit. Operational logs record request identifiers, routes, response status, and timing. Hosting providers may process network information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the service.

How information is used

Request information is used to return financial data, diagnose errors, protect the service, and improve reliability. Company and screening requests may be sent to upstream data sources. Do not include personal or confidential information in queries.

Cookies and client storage

The Rust documentation pages do not set login or advertising cookies. Your MCP client, terminal tools, and hosting providers may have their own storage and privacy practices.

Sharing and support

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by hosting and upstream service providers, or disclosed when required by law. GitHub issues are public: do not post passwords, personal data, or confidential logs. GitHub handles information submitted there under its own privacy statement.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure. Operational information is retained as needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the service and meet applicable legal obligations.

Requests and updates

To ask about access, correction, deletion, or another privacy concern, use the support page to request a private contact method without sharing personal information publicly. Updates to this policy will be published on this page.
